Generic LLMs talk. Decision models answer.

  • Bounded answers, not free text.

    A decision model is given the question and the allowed answers, and returns a probability for each in a single pass. It cannot wander off or invent an option, which is where much of the risk in generative output lives.

  • Confidence you can see.

    Calibrated probabilities show where the model is sure and where it is not. Confident answers can be pre-filled. Uncertain ones go to a person, with the evidence attached.

  • Small, fast, and yours to override.

    Compact models answer in a single pass and use a fraction of the energy of a large model, so routine questions can be answered before anyone has to ask. The clinician confirms or overrides every answer.

In a zero-shot radiology evaluation on a single workstation, a 3-billion-parameter decision model came within 0.04 AUROC of a 27B one on report findings while using 3 to 9 times less energy per request. Reading a generative model out as a decision model cut calibration error on CT volumes from 0.436 to 0.072. No model won every task, so models and thresholds should be chosen on local cases.
